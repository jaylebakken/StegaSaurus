The gap

Pre-release music moves faster than accountability.

Demos, masters, stems, and review copies move through collaborators, managers, labels, distributors, and sync buyers. File names and metadata are easy to lose. Saurus is designed for the moment after a leak appears: you know the song, and you need to identify which authorized copy came back.

Attribution layer

Find the copy, not just the song.

Audio-carried evidence

Each export carries attribution evidence designed for controlled verification against retained records.

Registry-backed identity

The audio points to an opaque handle. Recipient names, delivery notes, and custody history stay in the registry.

Known-song search

The product workflow starts with the suspected track, then checks which authorized export best matches the leak.

Honest uncertainty

Verification can accept, reject, or abstain when the evidence is not strong enough for a responsible match.

Deployment shape

A simple product path today, a real platform tomorrow.

Local desktop app

Import clean audio, create recipient exports, listen to results, and run known-song leak checks from a private local workflow.

Registry service

Store songs, opaque handles, recipient metadata, export history, and parent-child lineage outside the audio itself.

Verification reports

Turn accepted evidence, abstentions, crop position, and lineage into a readable investigation result for music teams.

Built for

Teams sharing unreleased music.

  • Producers and managers sending demos
  • Independent labels issuing promo copies
  • A&R teams reviewing sensitive masters
  • Sync and catalog teams sending previews

Product promise

A leak investigation in one clear path.

Register a track, create exports, simulate a leaked excerpt, and verify the matching recipient. The current Stegosaurus prototype demonstrates this workflow locally under named evaluations; broader production robustness requires further validation.
